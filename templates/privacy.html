Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

What We Collect

JalaDownloader does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not use cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics services.

Video Links

The URLs you submit for download are sent directly to the yt-dlp process running on this server. We do not log, store, or share these URLs with any third party.

Downloaded Files

Files you download are temporarily stored on the server and automatically deleted immediately after they are delivered to your browser. In the rare case of a failed download, files are cleaned up automatically within 30 minutes.

Third-Party Services

This tool uses yt-dlp (open-source) to process downloads. No data is sent to any third-party analytics or tracking services. The video content you download is retrieved directly from the source platform.

Data Retention

We retain no data. No logs, no download history, no user accounts. Every session is stateless.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Since this is a personal tool, changes will be minimal.

For questions, reach out via the contact page.
